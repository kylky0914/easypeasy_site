## About Palazon

`palazon.sg` did not resolve directly — the live company is **Palazon Technology Pte Ltd** at
**[palazon.com](https://palazon.com)** (Singapore, founded 1997).

**Focus:** AI transformation & automation, custom business software, and its own ERP (**PalaERP**).
Emphasizes Singapore-based senior engineers, EDG grant support, and local compliance
(IRAS, GST, PDPA, PEPPOL e-invoicing). Serves clients across 30+ countries in banking, airlines,
legal, automotive, HR, and government.
